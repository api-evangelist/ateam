# a.team

> Enterprise AI talent network that connects companies with elite tech builders to design, build, and scale AI products and solutions.

## About

a.team is a network of elite tech talent — senior engineers, product managers, designers, and data scientists — that helps companies build and ship AI products faster. The platform matches enterprises with pre-vetted expert teams for AI development, providing both dedicated talent and full-team engagements. a.team serves Fortune 500 companies and high-growth startups across industries including CPG, healthcare, financial services, legal, and government.
